FIRST CONTACT / STUDY INTAKE

Bring the image. Bring the context.

The X-ray is the starting point. The surrounding clinical information is what makes the interpretation useful.

Give me the X-ray — then give me the story around it.

Upload your X-ray and provide every relevant detail you have: age and sex, symptoms and when they started, the body part and side imaged, why the X-ray was ordered, medical and surgical history, medications, previous diagnoses, earlier X-rays or scans, radiology reports, laboratory results, and any other records connected to the problem. Then tell me the exact clinical question you want answered. The more complete the context, the better the analysis.
